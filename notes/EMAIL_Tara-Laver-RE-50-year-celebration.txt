I’d like to share info about an archives exhibit we just opened in the Spencer Art Reference Library at the Nelson-Atkins and an upcoming related program. I hope some of you can make it by, and please share with anyone you know who may be interested.

This year marks the 50th anniversary of the Kansas City showing of The Exhibition of Archaeological Finds of the People's Republic of China at the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art. In connection with this anniversary, the archives exhibit "50 Years On: Remembering The Exhibition of the Archaeological Finds of the People’s Republic of China" is on view in the library through June 2. The display explores how the exhibit was part of the effort to normalize relations with the People’s Republic of China, the exhibit itself, and the public's response to it.

Not only an important international diplomatic moment that illustrated the power of art to bring together peoples and nations, experiencing The Exhibition of the Archaeological Finds of the People’s Republic of China remains a common, cherished experience for many long-time museum goers.  After opening in Paris in 1973, the exhibit travelled to London, Vienna, Stockholm, Toronto, and Washington, D.C., before coming to the Nelson-Atkins and continuing to the Asian Art Museum of San Francisco.

Join us for a related program on May 4, 2:30-3:30.
